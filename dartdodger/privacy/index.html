Dart Dodger · iPhone game

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

The short version: Dart Dodger does not collect any information about you or your child.

  • No personal information is collected, stored or shared.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no in-app purchases.
  • The game does not use the internet.
  • The best score is saved only on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. Dart Dodger does not ask for, collect, record or transmit any personal information, such as a name, email address, phone number, photo, voice, location, contacts or device identifiers. It does not use advertising identifiers and does not track you across apps or websites.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps one number on your device: your best score, so it can show it the next time you play. This number never leaves the device. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to you in any way. Deleting the app removes it.

No ads, analytics or network access

Dart Dodger contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. All of the game's pictures and sounds are built into the app, and the game does not connect to the internet while you play.

The only link that leaves the game is a link to this privacy policy. It sits in the Parents area, behind a parental gate (an adult has to hold a button and answer a maths question), and it opens in a web browser.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Dart Dodger is made for children and families. We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws in other countries by not collecting personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing to review, share, sell or delete, and no parental consent is needed to play.

If you believe information about your child has somehow been sent to us, please contact us (see below) and we will delete it promptly.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share any information, because we do not have any.

Apple

You download Dart Dodger from Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy when you use the App Store or your device. That is outside the app's control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever changes what it collects, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
