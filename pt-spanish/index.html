PT Spanish for kids & families

Custom phrase bank for a pediatric physical therapist working with Latin American Spanish-speaking families—standing, sitting, swallowing, home exercises, and encouragement. Not a full course; short phrases you can tap and rehearse before a session.

Language practice only. This page is not medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical guidance. Use your professional judgment; confirm wording with a qualified interpreter when needed.

Convention: Latin American Spanish (e.g. computadora, not ordenador). Forms use usted with parents/caregivers for respect in a clinical setting. With the child you can soften tone; many phrases below work for both.
